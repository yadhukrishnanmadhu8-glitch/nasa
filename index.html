    Terrain is 200 m resolution, so slopes and hazards are regional-scale and miss boulders and small scarps. Ice values show how consistent the data are with buried ice, not a measured amount. The walking-speed model is an assumption. Temperatures are estimates: a −63 °C Mars-wide baseline, −0.001 °C per metre from the window’s mean elevation, and an assumed ±20 °C daily swing (Viking landers saw 35 to 50 K daily variation). Mineral rankings other than water-ice are estimates, not maps. Prototype, not for operations.
